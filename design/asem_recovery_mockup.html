?How to install a ROM

Install writes a file to your phone. Follow the steps in order.

1Back up first. Home › Backup › Boot + Vendor Boot + NVRAM.
2Wipe › Factory reset when you change to a different ROM.
3Choose ZIP, tap the ROM file, then Install.
4Swipe to start. Do not unplug or power off until it says done.
5Add GApps, then Magisk last — use Add to queue.
No file on the phone? Use ASEM Transfer to copy it from your PC, or Fastboot to flash from the PC.
